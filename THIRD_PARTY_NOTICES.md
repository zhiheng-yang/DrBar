## ccfddl conference data

Conference deadlines and acceptance rates come from [ccfddl](https://github.com/ccfddl/ccf-deadlines).
DrBar downloads them from <https://ccfddl.com/conference/allconf.yml> and
<https://ccfddl.com/conference/allacc.yml> at run time and bundles a snapshot of both for the first
launch without network. The data is shown as ccfddl lists it.
