Menu bar

菜单栏

Read it like a clock.

像看钟一样看它。

When the work starts, the whole outline.开始做的那天，整圈都有颜色。 One round is its time, from its first day to the deadline at the top.一圈就是它的时间，从第一天走到顶上的 DDL。

The colored stretch is the time left.有颜色的那一段，就是剩下的时间。 It shrinks toward the top as the day comes, like a timer.DDL 越近，它越往顶上缩，像计时器一样。

Within a week, it turns orange.一周之内，变成橙色。 So do the days beside the name.名字旁边的天数也一起变色。

Three days left: red, and a !只剩三天：变红，学位帽换成 ! It reads without color too.不看颜色也认得出来。

Pick a paper, and its plan goes round.指定一篇论文，绕着走的就是它的计划。 A stretch per block, in the block's own color.每个时间块一段，用它自己的颜色。

Solid is done. The dot is today.实心是做完的，圆点是今天。 The red mark is the deadline; the dot turns orange if work falls behind.红色刻度是 DDL；工作落后时，圆点变橙。

Color is how close

颜色表示远近

Calm while there is time, orange within a week, red within three days, when a red ! takes the place of the cap.

时间还多时是平静的颜色，一周内变橙，三天内变红，这时学位帽换成红色的 !。

A plan goes round

计划绕着走

Pick a paper and its blocks go round the frame in their colors: solid when done, faint still to do, a dot at today.

指定一篇论文，它的时间块就用各自的颜色绕着边框：做完的是实心，没做的是浅色，今天是一个小圆点。

Four ways to show it

四种样子

With the name, with the days too, the next two deadlines in two frames, or the symbol alone for a crowded menu bar.

只显示名字、加上天数、用两个边框显示接下来两个 DDL，或者菜单栏太挤时只显示图标。

Overview

总览

Your whole term,
on one axis.

整个学期，
一条时间轴。

Papers on top, then your own groups, then the conferences you follow. Pinch to zoom from a few days to two years: close in, every day has its column and the weekends are shaded, so the days left can be counted on the bars.

上面是论文，然后是你自己建的分组，最后是你关注的会议。双指捏合，在几天到两年之间缩放：放大后每天各占一格、周末有底色，还剩几天在横条上就能数出来。

Plans

计划

A paper is a few
stretches of time.

一篇论文，
就是几段时间。

Experiments, writing, internal review, buffer: each kind of work has its row, in as many rounds as it takes. Plan backwards from the deadline in one click, then draw and drag blocks as in Calendar.

实验、写作、内审修改、缓冲，每类工作一行，可以分好几轮。一键从 DDL 倒推出计划，再像"日历"一样画出、拖动时间块。

Draw like Calendar

像日历一样画

Drag across empty days to draw a block, drag its ends or its middle, hold ⌥ to copy.

在空白日子上拖出时间块，拖两端改长短、拖中间挪位置，按住 ⌥ 复制。

Plan backwards

从 DDL 倒推

Buffer, internal review, writing and experiments, counted back from the paper deadline.

从论文 DDL 往前排好缓冲、内审、写作和实验。

Milestones

里程碑

Internal deadlines and drafts for your supervisor, as diamonds on the plan.

内部 DDL、给导师的初稿，都是计划上的菱形。

Every deadline

每一个 DDL

Abstract, paper, rebuttal and notification, plus your own: supplementary, camera-ready.

摘要、论文、rebuttal、录用通知，还有你自己的：补充材料、camera-ready。

People on blocks

谁在做哪一段

Put your supervisor on the internal review and a co-author on a round of experiments.

内审标上导师，某一轮实验标上合作者。

Nothing lost

删不丢

⌘Z undoes; whatever you delete waits thirty days in Recently Deleted.

⌘Z 可以撤销；删掉的东西在"最近删除"里保留 30 天。

Groups

分组

And the rest of
your work too.

其他工作，
也放得下。

Applications, reviews, teaching: make groups of your own and plan their items like papers. Work that is only a deadline takes no row at all, just a flag on its group's line.

申请、审稿、助教：自己建分组，里面的事项像论文一样排计划。只有一个 DDL 的工作不占一行，只是分组那一行上的一面小旗。

添加工作、挪动时间块、问问接下来要交什么，用语音、聚焦搜索或"快捷指令"都行，中英文都可以。

Progress emails

进度邮件

Keep your supervisor
in the loop.

让导师
随时知道进展。

Send Progress writes the paper's timeline into an email from your own Mail account, as a reply in the same thread, with what matters in the first line. Nobody needs to install anything.

"发送进展"会用你自己的"邮件"账户，把论文的时间轴写进一封邮件，作为同一个对话里的回复，第一行就写明最要紧的事。对方什么都不用装。

Re: Progress: Efficient Video Diffusion (Writing · 26 days to the CVPR 2027 abstract deadline)

To: Prof. Jane Doe
收件人：Prof. Jane Doe

How to use

使用方法

Up and running
in five minutes.

五分钟，
就能用起来。

Install

安装

Download the zip, move DrBar to Applications and open it; the first time, click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. A graduation cap in a rounded frame appears in the menu bar.

下载 zip，把 DrBar 拖进"应用程序"并打开；第一次打开时，在 系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 里点"仍要打开"。菜单栏里会出现一个圆角边框，里面是学位帽。

Star your conferences

给会议标星

Click the frame, then Browse Conferences… (⌘F). Search, filter by rank, and star the ones you submit to.

点一下边框，再点"浏览会议…"（⌘F）。搜索、按等级筛选，给要投的会议标星。

Add a paper

添加论文

New Paper (⌘N), type its title, and Choose Conference… under it. Every date of the round becomes its deadline.

新建论文（⌘N），输入标题，在下面"选择会议…"。这一轮的每个日期都成了它的 DDL。

Plan it

排计划

Click Plan Backwards from Deadline, then drag blocks to fit your term. Click a block and press Delete to remove it.

点"从 DDL 倒推生成"，再拖动时间块排进你的日程。点选一个时间块按 Delete 就能删掉。

Glance at the menu bar

瞄一眼菜单栏

The colored stretch of the frame is the time left. Right-click a paper and Show in Menu Bar to watch its plan go round.

边框上有颜色的那一段就是剩下的时间。右键一篇论文选"在菜单栏显示"，就能看它的计划绕着走。

Tick off and share

完成一段，发给导师

Tick blocks off as you go, ask Siri what's next, and Send Progress to your supervisor when it's time.

做完一段勾一段，问 Siri 接下来是什么，需要时给导师"发送进展"。

Privacy

隐私

Your plans stay
on your Mac.

你的计划，
只留在你的 Mac 上。

Offline
离线可用

Everything works without a connection. The only download is the ccfddl conference data.

没有网络也一切照常。唯一的下载是 ccfddl 的会议数据。

No account
无需账户

No sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry. Email goes out from your own Mail, only when you press Send.

不用登录，没有统计和遥测。邮件从你自己的"邮件"发出，只在你点发送时。

One app
一个 App

No installer, no helper. Remove All Data empties everything it stored.

没有安装器，没有附带程序。"移除所有数据"会清空它存过的一切。

Free. Open source.
